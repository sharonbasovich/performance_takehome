# MI355X development measurements, 28 October 2026

These are actual Qwen3.6-27B measurements on one AMD MI355X, using the candidate
benchmark and evaluator. They are development results, not an organizer grade.
The calibration and quick captures, complete request evidence, reports and
independent timing audits were exported and hash-verified off the GPU.

| Capture | Measured requests | Warmups | Cold launches | Result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Calibration | 60 | 8 | 1 | Valid; separate deadline calibration frozen |
| Quick | 242 / 242 | 28 | 1 | Valid; no failed or incomplete requests |
| Full | In progress | In progress | 2 | Completion and final report pending |

The quick capture covered eight workload cells. Its cold start to first valid
generation was **25.287 seconds**. The runner, evaluator, independent timing
audit, cleanup and export took **776.387 seconds**; this excludes earlier setup,
which remains counted from the original overall start of 16:10:34 UTC.

These workload comparisons describe the observed workloads; they are not a
configuration speedup. The A/A fluidity and decode controls reported
`no_difference`; TTFT p50 and p90 remained `inconclusive` at this sample size.
One quick repetition and one cold launch do not establish tail latency or
startup stability. The full population is retained for the longer run.

the attempts are never pooled. The source now incorporates owned-container
supervision and a Linux regression test for two consecutive cold-launch stops.
