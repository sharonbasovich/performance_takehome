# MI355X development measurements, 28 October 2026

Calibration, quick and full captures completed on one AMD MI355X with
Qwen3.6-27B and a digest-bound LLMD wrapper. These are candidate development
measurements, not an organizer grade. The table records the original capture-era
verdicts. Later quality/calibration reanalysis reproduces the same results; the
historical timing limitation below remains. The complete compressed raw captures,
reports, calibrated plan, independent timing audits and provenance are in the
[public replay packet](validation/real-run-20261028/README.md).

All three runner, evaluator and independent timing audits exited zero. Full had
zero protocol errors, evidence-invalid, unfinished or unoffered requests. Its two
failures are real `invalid_output:nonce_missing` responses at
`closed_loop_sweep|conc=32|rep0/measure/18` and
`closed_loop_sweep|conc=32|rep1/measure/0`; they remain in the offered population
and receive zero fluidity. TTFT and FTGR condition on completed valid traces.
`Valid` is the original evaluator's result on its recorded evidence; it does not
assert perfect output or coverage of the later repaired boundary-lag gate.

**Historical timing limit:** the full recorder reported a filtered maximum
event-loop lag of 8.51089 ms, but retained no raw lag intervals or unfiltered

by that recorder. Its absence cannot be established retrospectively. Recorder
1.1.1 fixes this observation rule for future captures; the later replay does not
reconstruct missing lag evidence or certify the historical run against that gate.

These are descriptive workload results, not a configuration speedup. Metrics
conditioned on valid traces must be read with completion counts; failures are
never silently removed from the offered population. Fluidity includes its
prefill deadline, so it is not a pure decode-throughput score.

Full A/A control A and B each contributed 90 observations. Strict fluidity,
minimum-decode-deadline p99 and TTFT p90 reported `no_difference`; TTFT p50 was
`inconclusive`, making the overall A/A decision **inconclusive**. Quick A/A had
fluidity and minimum-decode `no_difference`, with both TTFT comparisons
`inconclusive`. The report uses 2,000 request-level bootstrap draws, 98.75%
adjusted confidence and pools repetitions within cell/load. Its sparse tails,
IID assumption and lack of cluster correction limit inference. This does not
establish measured power, population coverage or a finite-sample familywise
error guarantee. No speedup, power or official grading claim is made.

The separate 60-request calibration fit covered approximate prompt lengths
44–4117; the scored plan spans 44–4081. Its nonnegative line plus maximum
observed positive residual is an observed envelope, not a prediction interval.
The plan was frozen before scoring and never refitted on quick/full data.

The first full attempt stopped before measured requests: its attached Docker
client exited while the engine remained alive, so the second cold launch
correctly refused the warm responder. That failed attempt and earlier October
26 work retain their own history and are never pooled with this full capture.

regressions cover two consecutive stops and cancellation during creation.

preserving generic whitespace tolerance; require calibration plan coverage;
